Tooth pain? Start here

Root canal treatment

When the inside of a tooth gets infected or badly inflamed, it throbs, reacts to hot and cold, or swells. A root canal cleans out the infection so the tooth can stay in your mouth instead of being pulled out.

  • Numbing first, so you're comfortable throughout
  • The infected inside is cleaned and sealed
  • Usually followed by a crown to protect the tooth

Strength + looks

Crowns

A crown is a cap that fits over a damaged tooth. It protects a weak or cracked tooth, covers one after a root canal, and brings back its shape so you can chew properly again.

  • Tooth is shaped and measured for a precise fit
  • Colour matched to your other teeth
  • Brings back biting strength and a natural look
Ask about crowns
For missing teeth

Dental implants

If you've lost a tooth, an implant replaces it from the root up. A small titanium post goes into the jaw, and a crown sits on top. It's the closest thing to getting your own tooth back.

  • Assessment first, to see if an implant suits you
  • Stable, fixed and doesn't slip like a denture
  • Step by step plan, explained before we begin
Ask about implants
Straighter smile

Braces

Crooked, crowded or gapped teeth are harder to clean and can affect your bite. Braces move them gently into place over time. It's not just for teenagers; adults get them too.

  • Consultation to look at your bite and goals
  • Regular short adjustment visits, easy to fit in evenings
  • Tips on cleaning around braces so teeth stay healthy

Scaling & polishing

Plaque hardens into tartar that brushing can't remove. It stains teeth, causes bad breath and irritates gums. Scaling lifts it off and polishing leaves your teeth smooth and fresh.

  • Quick visit, you can come straight after work
  • Helps with stains, gum bleeding and bad breath
  • A good habit every six months or so
